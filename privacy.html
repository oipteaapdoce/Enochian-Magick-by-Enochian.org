Privacy Policy — Enochian Grimoire Companion

Last updated: October 2026

Enochian Grimoire Companion collects no data about you.

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
  • No network use. The app makes no network requests. Nothing leaves your device.
  • No location. The app does not use GPS, location services, or any location data.
  • No account. The app requires no account, no sign-in, and no registration.
  • No ads. No tracking. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no trackers.

On-device storage only. Anything you enter in the app — your Aethyre travel journal, 19-day planner checkboxes, and temple checklist ticks — is stored only in your own device's local storage, inside the app itself. It is never sent anywhere, and deleting the app removes it.

Publisher. Enochian Grimoire Companion is published by Enochian Alchemical Magick. Website: https://www.enochian.org.
