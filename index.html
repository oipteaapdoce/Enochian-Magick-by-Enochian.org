Enochian Grimoire Companion

A working reference from Athena Wallinder's Enochian Grimoire (2nd ed., 2026)

About this app, read first

This companion is the at-hand reference to Enochian Grimoire by Athena Wallinder (2nd edition, 2026). It holds the tables, names, calculators, and checklists a practitioner wants on their phone. The teaching itself stays in the book.

Version 1 honesty note. Transcriptions in v1 are as-is from the book and from Casaubon 1659, unproofread. The book's image-only tables (the 91 Parts, the squares) were transcribed from page images; uncertain readings are flagged [?]. Alphabet glyphs come from the Glumbosch Enochian font (OFL), verified against Athena's reference chart; Ged and Tal are traced from the chart. Athena's proofread readings and her own versions of the Calls come in later versions. Nothing here was copied from any modern translation or edition.

Sources. Every section names its source: book section, or Casaubon 1659 where used. Full texts of Calls 1–18 are from Sloane MS 3191; the 19th Call and the first words are from Casaubon 1659 (see Keys tab). Cacodemons are not in the free v1, see the Cacodemons tab.

§18 note. The book asks readers to keep silent about what is taught in this grimoire. This app covers reference material the author has chosen to include; the advanced Part 3 material stays in the book.

How to use it

  • Great Table, the grid, the derivation rules, and the directional-layout dispute, labelled.
  • Bonorum, the 7 Kings + 7 Princes, weekday ruler, Governor-of-the-Hour.
  • Alphabet, the 21 letters, glyph, name, sound.
  • Keys, the 19 Calls: correspondence table, first words of all 19, full texts of all 19.
  • Aethyres, the 30 Aethyres, pacing rules, travel journal.
  • 91 Parts, the author's own geographic mapping: part, governor, direction, tribe/sign, place.
  • Squares, the 7 Bonorum squares: purpose, material, method, grid.
  • Temple, build checklist.
  • Workings, 19-day program planner and sigil tracer.
  • Finder, which entity to call for what.
  • Cacodemons, placeholder for a later paid version.

Source: Enochian Grimoire §1–2. Grid as printed in the book.

The grid

Tap a cell to see its row/column and which quarter it belongs to. The Table is read in four quarters, each ruled by its King, Seniors, and Angels.

English shows the grid as printed, capitals as in the book. Enochian renders each letter in its Enochian glyph, Enochian has no upper/lower case, so capitals are shown as their letter. Ged (G) and Tal (M) are traced from the reference chart; all other glyphs are the Glumbosch font. Both shows glyph + English letter.
